Privacy Policy

OpenHand: Gym Journal

Last updated: May 15, 2026

Overview

OpenHand: Gym Journal ("the App") is designed to respect your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Data Storage

All workout data, preferences, and chat history are stored locally on your device. The App does not operate any external servers or databases. If you delete the App, all associated data will be removed.

Google Sign-In and AI Coach Feature

The App offers an optional AI Coach feature powered by Google Gemini. To use this feature, you sign in with your Google account via OAuth 2.0. When you sign in, we receive:

  • An access token to authenticate requests to Google Gemini on your behalf
  • A refresh token to maintain your session

These tokens are stored securely in your device's keychain and are never transmitted to any server other than Google's. We do not access your Google account data, contacts, emails, or files.

Data Sent to Google Gemini

When you use the AI Coach feature, the following data is sent to Google Gemini to generate responses:

  • Your chat messages
  • Your last 30 days of workout history (exercises, sets, reps, and weights)
  • Your personal records

This data is sent directly from your device to Google's servers. The App does not intercept, store, or relay this data through any intermediary. Google's use of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Gemini API Terms of Service.

Data We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our own servers. We do not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We have no access to your workout data, chat conversations, or Google account information.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
