Overview

OpenHand: Gym Journal ("the App") is designed to respect your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data you enter into the App is stored locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Storage

All workout data and preferences are stored locally on your device. If you delete the App, all associated data will be removed.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.
