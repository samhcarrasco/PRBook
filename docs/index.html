PRBook

OpenHand: Gym Journal

About

PRBook is a workout journal app for iOS that helps you track exercises, sets, reps, and personal records. It includes an optional AI Coach feature powered by Google Gemini that provides personalized training advice based on your workout history.

Features

Log workouts, track progress over time, view personal records, and get AI-powered coaching — all stored locally on your device.
